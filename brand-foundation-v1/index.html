Phase 01

Before we design a logo or pick a palette, we need to answer three questions that will anchor every creative decision that follows.

Internal

External

Phase 03

Who Is It For?

We serve two audiences. One is primary — the people we build for first. The other is secondary — the people who benefit indirectly but are essential to the ecosystem.

Primary Audience

Builders & Creators

People who want to build agents, skills, and tools — but don't want to manage infrastructure.

Jobs to Be Done

  • Build an AI agent without writing boilerplate
  • Connect that agent to the tools and data it needs
  • Share skills and MCPs with the community
  • Monetize their work without a middleman taking 30%
  • Discover what other people have already built
Secondary Audience

Organizations & Teams

Companies and teams that want to deploy AI agents at scale — with governance, observability, and team collaboration.

Jobs to Be Done

  • Orchestrate multiple agents across a workflow
  • Govern access, cost, and compliance
  • Integrate agents into existing toolchains
  • Measure agent performance and ROI
  • Scale from prototype to production

Phase 05

Messaging Hierarchy

How we talk about Starchild — from the shortest tagline to the full story.

Tagline

Where intention meets machine.

Elevator Pitch

Starchild helps builders create AI agents and share skills in a connected marketplace — no infrastructure headaches, no middlemen taking cuts.

About Paragraph

Starchild is a brand foundation for the AI-native builder. We provide Conductor Mode — a unified interface for creating, connecting, and deploying AI agents — alongside a marketplace for skills and MCPs. Our mission is to make the connection between human intention and machine capability as simple as possible, so builders can focus on what matters: the work they care about.

Full Story

We believe every person has a unique light — a star inside them — that deserves a clear path to the world. Starchild exists to remove the friction between what you want to build and what you're actually able to build. We've spent years in the trenches of AI infrastructure, and we know how lonely and expensive the old way is. That's why we built Starchild: a foundation-first platform where builders compose from proven blocks, connect to the tools they need, share their work with the community, and keep the rewards of their labor. This isn't just software. It's a different way of building together.

Phase 06

Voice Principles

How we sound — the principles, the examples, and the words we choose (and avoid).

01

Warm, not corporate

✓ Do

"Hey, let's build something together."

✕ Don't

"We leverage synergistic paradigms to optimize outcomes."

02

Clear, not clever

✓ Do

"Connect your agent to any tool with one line."

✕ Don't

"Unlock the full potential of our paradigm-shifting MCP ecosystem."

03

Confident, not arrogant

✓ Do

"We built the hard parts so you don't have to."

✕ Don't

"No one else can do what we do. We're the only choice."

04

Human, not robotic

✓ Do

"We're still learning. Tell us what you need."

✕ Don't

"Your feedback is valuable to us. Please submit a support ticket."

Words we use

build connect light share together clear simple star path craft open home

Words we avoid

synergy leverage paradigm disrupt optimize enterprise seamless best-in-class cutting-edge robust
Phase 07

Visual Direction

Where we are now, where we're heading, and what comes later. Visual decisions are staged so we don't over-invest before we know what works.

Locked Now

What's locked

  • Primary colors: Yellow (#FFD166), Magenta (#E63984), Blue (#118AB2)
  • Base tone: Near-black ink (#1A1A2E) on warm paper (#FAF6F0)
  • System fonts: Editorial serif for headings, geometric sans for body
  • Logo mark: The star (✦) as primary symbol — connection point, light, anchor
  • Spacing rhythm: 8px grid, generous whitespace
Directional

What's directional

  • Logo lockup (wordmark + mark combinations)
  • Icon system for product surfaces
  • Photography / illustration style
  • Motion principles (subtle, purposeful)
  • Component library (buttons, cards, inputs)
Later

What's later

  • Full design system with tokens
  • Brand film / motion identity
  • Merchandise & physical collateral
  • Third-party partner guidelines
  • Localized visual adaptations

Phase 10

Landing Page Validation Loop

Before we build the full site, we validate the core message with a minimal landing page. This is the loop: build → test → learn → iterate.

1

Build

Create a single-page landing with the tagline, elevator pitch, and a waitlist sign-up. Nothing else.

→
2

Test

Drive real traffic. Watch where people click, where they leave, what they sign up for.

→
3

Learn

What landed? What confused? What made them stay? Document the signals.

→
4

Iterate

Refine the message based on evidence, not opinion. Then run the loop again.

Rule: We don't ship a full brand until the landing page proves the message resonates. The loop doesn't stop until it does.
Phase 11

After the LP — Brand System v1

Once the landing page validates our core message, we expand into a full brand system. This is what v1 looks like.

✦

Logo System

Primary mark, wordmark, combinations, clear-space rules, minimum sizes, misuse examples.

◈

Color System

Primary palette, secondary palette, accessibility contrast ratios, dark mode tokens.

◎

Typography System

Heading hierarchy, body styles, code blocks, display treatments, type scale.

⟡

Icon System

Product icons, UI icons, decorative marks — all in the Starchild visual language.

◇

Component Library

Buttons, cards, inputs, navigation, modals — all branded, all accessible.

△

Motion Principles

Transition speeds, easing curves, purposeful animation, reduced-motion defaults.
